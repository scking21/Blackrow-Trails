How do I ask about a trail on iPhone?

In Blackrow Trails 1.0 for iPhone, open a trail guide and tap Ask about this trail when available. On a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence enabled, the answer uses the guide’s trail details and is generated on your device.

With Apple’s ChatGPT extension enabled on a supported iPhone, open Planner → Ask a trail question and choose Use ChatGPT with iOS Writing Tools, then select the text in your temporary draft and choose Writing Tools → Compose. You choose what to send. Read about iPhone privacy and Apple Writing Tools.

What does Trail Pro cost on iPhone?

Blackrow Trails 1.0 for iPhone has no upfront charge. Trail Pro is optional. In the U.S., the monthly plan is $4.99 and the yearly plan is $39.99. Apple shows the price and any offer you qualify for before you confirm. Both plans renew automatically until canceled. Trying Trail Pro in the web browser does not start a paid store subscription.

My Trail Pro purchase is not showing

Use Restore purchases in the Trail Pro screen while signed in to the same Apple or Google account used for the purchase. If it still does not appear, email us with the platform and the error you see.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store where you subscribed. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, select your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, select your profile, then Payments & subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I use the app without a connection?

Prepare your trip and use Trail Pro to download the maps you need before leaving service. Live weather and other online information require a connection. Check that your saved maps are available before starting your trip.

Where are my trips and reservations stored?

Trips and reservations are stored on your device. Use the app's backup and export controls before clearing app storage or uninstalling. We do not have a server copy of your trip database to restore for you.

Where do land boundaries come from?

The website and Blackrow Trails 1.0 for iPhone use the USGS Protected Areas Database for public lands and conservation easements. Parcel outlines are available in Colorado. A boundary on the map does not establish a right to enter. Check access with the land manager. Map data sources and credits.

Trail information or a location looks wrong

Tell us which trail or feature is affected and what appears incorrect. Trail and map conditions can change; follow posted closures and use current local information when planning your route.
