How can we help?

Email blackrowtech@gmail.com for help with Blackrow Trails.

Include your device model, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. Please leave out passwords, reservation documents, and precise location details.

My Trail Pro purchase is not showing

Use Restore purchases in the Trail Pro screen while signed in to the same Apple or Google account used for the purchase. If it still does not appear, email us with the platform and the error you see.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store where you subscribed. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, select your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, select your profile, then Payments & subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I use the app without a connection?

Prepare your trip and use Trail Pro to download the maps you need before leaving service. Live weather and other online information require a connection. Check that your saved maps are available before starting your trip.

Where are my trips and reservations stored?

Trips and reservations are stored on your device. Use the app's backup and export controls before clearing app storage or uninstalling. We do not have a server copy of your trip database to restore for you.

Trail information or a location looks wrong

Tell us which trail or feature is affected and what appears incorrect. Trail and map conditions can change; follow posted closures and use current local information when planning your route.
