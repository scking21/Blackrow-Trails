🔒 Privacy & data

Your location stays on your device. Plans, waypoints, recorded tracks, and saved trails are stored only on this phone — never uploaded. Your live GPS is used on-device to center the map, record, and warn you off-route; it is never sent to a server.

  • The map, land, weather, and forecast layers fetch live data for the area you’re viewing or the point you tap — that reveals what you look at to those data providers, but not where you are.
  • Backups and GPX files you export contain precise coordinates — only you choose when to share them.
  • If the toggle below is on, the app sends anonymous usage events and crash reports (e.g. “trial started”, “offline download used”) so we can improve it. These never include your location, tracks, plans, or any identity — no profiles, no IP location lookup — and are never sold or shared with advertisers. A Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control signal on your device turns this off automatically.
