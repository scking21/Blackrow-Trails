Privacy & data

Your precise GPS fix is processed on this device and is not sent to Blackrow Trails or analytics. Plans, waypoints, recorded tracks, and saved trails stay on this phone unless you export or share them. Map and weather providers receive the area you view, which can match your location when you center the map there.

  • The map, land, weather, and forecast layers send the viewed area or selected point to their data providers. If you center the map on your position, that request can approximate where you are.
  • Backups and GPX files you export contain precise coordinates — only you choose when to share them.
  • If the toggle below is on, the app sends limited usage events and redacted crash reports to PostHog. These include a random installation ID and basic app and device details. App events exclude coordinates, tracks, plans, reservation documents, and user-entered content. Session recording and person profiles are disabled, and events request no IP-based location enrichment. A Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal turns analytics off automatically.
