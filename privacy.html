Blackrow Trails Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-23

Blackrow Trails ("the app") is private by default. The app has no Blackrow account system and shows no ads. Most trip, reservation, and location data stays on your device. The app contacts third-party services for maps, weather, subscriptions, optional analytics, and features that you choose to open or share, including optional assistant-generated trip transfers. We do not sell personal data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following data in its local app storage:

  • Your trip plan (trails you've added) and Trip Kit selections
  • Waypoints you mark
  • Tracks you record (the GPS path you walked, with elevations and timestamps)
  • Trails you save from a recording
  • Your map-mode and gear-list preferences
  • Travel reservations (flights, hotels, car rentals, trains) you add, and any e-ticket or itinerary documents you upload for them
  • Downloaded maps, map caches, membership, trial, reviewer-access, and privacy settings
  • Trip packages awaiting import, including their source provider and integrity metadata

Blackrow Trails does not upload this data to a Blackrow account. Data leaves your device only when a feature described below needs a third-party service or when you choose to export, open, or share it.

Location data

The app accesses precise location to center the map, record a track, place a waypoint, point the augmented-reality marker, and warn you when you leave a followed route. Blackrow Trails does not send the raw GPS fix to the developer or to PostHog.

When you center the map on your position or request nearby map, trail, parcel, elevation, radar, or weather data, the provider receives the requested map area or point. That area or point can approximate your location. The provider also receives the IP address and standard request metadata needed for an internet connection.

The app requests location while you use it. If you enable Keep recording with the screen off, the app requests background location so that it can continue an active recording. This setting is off by default. You can revoke location access in your device settings.

The camera is used only for the augmented-reality view. The app does not save or send camera images.

Travel reservations

When you add a reservation by uploading an e-ticket or itinerary PDF or screenshot, the app reads and parses the file on your device. Text extraction and optical character recognition run locally. The original document and the extracted reservation data remain in the app's local database unless you export or share them.

Trip imports and assistant transfers

Trip files that you select for manual import are read and reviewed on your device. Blackrow Trails does not upload a manually selected trip file.

If you open a supported Blackrow Trails transfer link created through an AI assistant or another supported provider, the native app stores the one-time transfer token in the device's Keychain or Keystore. The app sends that token to transfer.blackrowtrails.com to claim the trip package, downloads the assistant-generated trip content and source-provider metadata, and sends a lease acknowledgment after the package is saved. The transfer service also receives your IP address and standard request metadata. This flow does not upload your existing Blackrow Trails trips, reservations, tracks, or raw GPS location.

The received package is stored in the app's local SQLite database while it awaits your review. Transfer tokens, leases, trip content, trip identifiers, and content hashes are excluded from PostHog analytics. The AI assistant or other provider used to create the package may process the information that you give it under its own privacy policy before the package reaches Blackrow Trails.

Maps, weather, and external services

To show live maps and conditions, the app fetches data for the area that you are viewing or the point that you tap from third-party providers:

  • Map tiles: OpenTopoMap, OpenStreetMap, and USGS Topo
  • Elevation tiles: AWS Terrarium
  • Public land / parcels: USGS PAD-US and county/state parcel services (Esri/ArcGIS)
  • Trails & features: USGS National Map, OpenStreetMap/Overpass
  • Weather: the U.S. National Weather Service for forecasts, RainViewer for radar, and the Iowa Environmental Mesonet as a radar fallback

When a live source is down or you are offline, the app may reuse data it fetched earlier for the same area. This reuse involves no network request.

The app sends a bounding box, tile coordinate, or selected point as needed. It does not add your name, email address, Blackrow account identifier, or raw GPS fix. Each provider receives your IP address and standard request metadata and handles the request under its own privacy policy.

If you open directions, permit information, or another external link, the selected website or maps app receives the destination in that link. Blackrow Trails does not control data after another app or website receives it.

Usage analytics and crash reports

When analytics is enabled, the app sends limited usage events and redacted error reports to PostHog in the United States at us.i.posthog.com:

  • What is sent: which screens and features are used, taps on app controls (with all on-screen text and attributes masked), app version and device type, operating system, and technical error details. Each install is identified by a random ID that is not connected to a Blackrow account.
  • Excluded content. The app removes location-shaped fields, coordinates, tracks, waypoints, routes, plans, place names, transfer codes, trip identifiers, content hashes, and similar values from app events. Recorded tracks, reservations, uploaded documents, exports, and user-entered content are not intentionally included in analytics. Error messages and stack traces are redacted before transmission.
  • Limited profiling. Session recording is disabled. PostHog person profiles are disabled. Every event requests that PostHog disable IP-based location enrichment. PostHog still receives the network request and may process its IP address to provide its service.
  • Never for advertising. This data is used only to make product decisions (what to build, fix, or remove). It is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers, and we show no ads.
  • Your choice. Analytics is on by default unless your device sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. The app shows a notice on first launch. Turn analytics off at any time at Planner > Privacy & data > Share limited usage & redacted crash reports. Your choice is stored on your device and takes effect immediately. Deleting local app data rotates the random installation identifier.

PostHog processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy policy.

Subscriptions and purchases

Google Play or Apple processes Trail Pro purchases and subscriptions. The app receives product, purchase, receipt, and entitlement status needed to show offers, unlock Trail Pro, and restore a purchase. Promotional and offer codes are entered and processed in the platform-owned Google Play or Apple flow. The app receives the resulting receipt and entitlement status, not your full payment-card or bank-account details. Google or Apple may associate the transaction and code redemption with your store account under its own privacy policy. If analytics is enabled, the app may record that a promotional code was redeemed and the mobile platform used, but not the code itself.

A private access code may be provided to Google Play's review team. The app normalizes and hashes that code on the device, compares it with a stored hash, and saves only whether review access was granted. The entered review code is not sent to Blackrow Trails, PostHog, Google Play, or Apple by this check.

Files you export

Backups and GPX exports contain precise coordinates, and recorded-track GPX can include timestamps (when you were at each point). These files are created only when you choose to export or share them. The receiving app, person, or service controls the copy after you share it. To omit timestamps from recorded-track GPX files, enable Strip timestamps from exported track GPX before exporting.

Security

Local data is stored in the operating system's app sandbox. Internet requests made by the app use HTTPS. Native trip-transfer tokens and leases are stored in the device's Keychain or Keystore rather than the trip database. The app limits analytics fields and redacts sensitive values from error reports before transmission. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you delete it, clear the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstall the app. Use the app's item-level controls to delete individual trips and reservations.

Planner > Privacy & data > Delete local field data and settings clears the app's local settings, waypoints, recorded tracks, saved trails, gear choices, membership state, and analytics identifier. This control does not erase the SQLite trip and reservation database. To remove that database and the app's cached data, clear Blackrow Trails storage in your device settings or uninstall the app.

Deleting app data does not delete copies that you previously exported or shared. Google and Apple retain store transaction records under their own policies.

A pending assistant transfer token remains in secure device storage until the claim is completed and acknowledged, the transfer is cancelled, or the token is rejected as expired or already used. A received trip package remains in the local SQLite database until it is cancelled or app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Clearing app storage or uninstalling also removes the local review-access flag. It does not delete records retained by Google, Apple, an AI assistant, or the transfer service under their own policies.

We retain PostHog analytics only while needed to understand app reliability and feature use, then delete or aggregate data that is no longer needed. Because the app has no account and analytics is not tied to your name or email address, we cannot locate a specific person's past analytics events. Turning analytics off stops future collection. Deleting local app data rotates the random installation identifier.

Permissions

  • Location while in use: center the map, record or follow a route, use the augmented-reality view, and place waypoints.
  • Background location: optional; requested only if you enable Keep recording with the screen off, and used only to continue an active track recording in the background.
  • Camera: used only for the augmented-reality view. No photos are stored or sent.

Children

Blackrow Trails is a general-audience outdoor app and is not directed to children under 13. If analytics is enabled, the app handles the limited device and usage data described in this policy.

Policy changes

We update the date at the top of this policy when the app's data practices or this policy change materially.
